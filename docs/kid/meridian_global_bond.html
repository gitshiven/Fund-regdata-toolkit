What happens if the manufacturer is unable to pay out?

The assets of the fund are held separately by the depositary and are not available to the creditors of the manufacturer. You may still face a financial loss if the value of the underlying assets falls. There is no compensation or guarantee scheme covering that loss.

How long should I hold it and can I take money out early?

Recommended holding period: 3 years. The product is designed for investors who can leave their money invested for that period. Redeeming earlier increases the cost impact, as the table above shows.
